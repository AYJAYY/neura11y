# Accessible Slide Layout and Reading Order Guide

Slide accessibility depends heavily on titles, placeholders, and reading order.

---

## Use Built-In Layouts

Built-in layouts preserve a sensible structure for:

- slide title
- body content
- lists
- media placeholders

Manual text boxes and shapes often create confusing reading order.

---

## Title Every Slide

- Every slide needs a unique title.
- If the title should not be visible, keep it in the slide and hide it visually rather than deleting it.

---

## Selection Pane Reading Order

PowerPoint reads objects from the bottom of the Selection Pane to the top.

Recommended order:

1. Title
2. Main content
3. Supporting objects
4. Decorative objects

---

## Complex Visuals

For charts, diagrams, and maps:

- keep a short alt text on the visual
- put the full explanation in speaker notes when needed
- make sure the notes are included in the sharing workflow if the visual is essential
