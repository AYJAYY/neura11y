# Readability Guide

Readability is one part of plain language. It does not replace user testing or content review, but it helps identify writing that is too dense for the intended audience.

---

## Common Readability Targets

| Audience | Target Grade Level | Flesch Reading Ease |
|----------|--------------------|---------------------|
| General public | Grade 6-8 | 60-70 |
| Healthcare and public instructions | Grade 6 | 65+ |
| Technical professional audience | Grade 10-12 | 45-55 |
| Legal or regulatory content | Grade 12-14 | 30-45 |

---

## Practical Writing Targets

- Average sentence length under 20 words
- Short paragraphs
- Active voice
- Common vocabulary
- One main idea per sentence

---

## Useful Tools

- Hemingway App
- Microsoft Word readability statistics
- Readable.io
- other reading-level calculators as rough screening tools

---

## Important Caveat

A lower grade score does not automatically mean the content is clearer. Technical content can still be hard to use if:

- headings are vague
- instructions are out of order
- jargon is undefined
- recovery steps are missing
