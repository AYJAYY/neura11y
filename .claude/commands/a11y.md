## Step 4 — Produce Guidance

Apply the prompt template loaded in Step 2 to the user's task. Follow the template's instructions exactly. Also enforce these output rules:

**At the top of your response:** One sentence stating which pattern matched and why.

**Citations:** Every WCAG success criterion must be cited as `SC X.X.X — [Title] (Level A|AA|AAA)`. Every EN 301 549 or Section 508 reference must include the clause number. Every ISO 9241-171 reference must include the section number.

**Normative vs. Prescriptive:** Prefix each requirement with `[NORMATIVE]` (what the standard requires) or `[PRESCRIPTIVE]` (recommended best practice from a domain guide). Never present an advisory WCAG technique as normative.

**Severity (audits only):**
- `[FAIL]` — Definitive standards violation
- `[WARN]` — Probable issue requiring manual verification
- `[BEST PRACTICE]` — Not a failure but a recommended improvement

**Legal outputs:** Include an issue-to-framework mapping table. Flag items requiring legal or compliance counsel. Do not provide legal advice — identify compliance implications only.

**Freshness:** If a file you read has `stale: true` in its frontmatter, add: "Note: This source is marked stale — verify against current documentation before acting." Platform guides with `last_verified` more than 6 months before 2026-04-29 should include: "Platform UI details may have changed — confirm character limits and flows directly on the platform."

**Uncertainty:** If uncertain about a specific requirement, say so explicitly rather than presenting a guess as fact.

**End every response** with a "Manual Testing Required" section listing what cannot be verified by static analysis alone.
