# Disability and Accessibility Statistics

**Note:** Statistics in this area vary by study methodology, country, and year. Use ranges rather than single numbers where precision is needed. Verify against current WHO, CDC, and government sources.

## Business Case Context

- **$490 billion** — Estimated annual US household income controlled by working-age adults with disabilities (American Institutes for Research)
- **$13 trillion** — Global annual disposable income of people with disabilities (Accenture, 2018)
- **Access to 25% more potential customers** — Rough estimate when accessibility barriers are removed
- **Captions benefit ~80% of viewers** — Used in noisy environments, non-native speakers, improved comprehension (Verizon Media study, 2019)
- **Search engine optimization** — Many accessibility improvements (heading structure, alt text, transcripts) also improve SEO
