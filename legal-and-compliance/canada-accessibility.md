# Canada Accessibility Laws Overview

**IMPORTANT:** Legal information changes. Verify with current official sources. Last reviewed: 2026-03-13.

---

## Federal: Accessible Canada Act (ACA) 2019

The **Accessible Canada Act (ACA)** (Bill C-81) came into force on July 11, 2019. It applies to federally regulated organizations.

### Scope

Applies to:
- Federal departments and agencies
- Crown corporations (CBC, Canada Post, etc.)
- Parliament and parliamentary agencies
- Federally regulated private sector (banks, airlines, railways, interprovincial trucking, telecommunications)

### Key Requirements

1. **Accessibility plans** — Organizations must proactively develop and publish accessibility plans
2. **Progress reports** — Annual reports on plan progress
3. **Feedback processes** — Mechanisms for people to submit accessibility feedback
4. **Employment, built environment, ICT, communications, procurement** — Plans must address all these areas

### ICT and Digital Accessibility

For ICT: the ACA directs the development of regulations specifying accessibility standards. The **Accessibility Standards Canada** organization develops technical standards.

**Federal web content:** Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat Standard on Web Accessibility requires **WCAG 2.0 Level AA** for federal government websites.

## Ontario: AODA (Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act) 2005

AODA is Canada's most comprehensive provincial legislation. It applies to public and private sector organizations in Ontario.

### IASR — Information and Communications Standards

The Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (IASR) under AODA includes web content requirements:

| Sector | Requirement | Deadline |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Government of Ontario | WCAG 2.0 Level AA | January 2016 |
| Large organizations (50+ employees) | WCAG 2.0 Level AA | January 2021 |
| Small private organizations (1–49 employees) | WCAG 2.0 Level A | January 2014 |
| Intranet/internal websites | WCAG 2.0 Level AA | January 2021 (large) |

### Other Provinces

| Province | Law | Status |
|----------|-----|--------|
| Manitoba | AMA (Accessibility for Manitobans Act) 2013 | In force; customer service standards apply |
| Nova Scotia | Accessibility Act 2017 | Phased implementation |
| British Columbia | Accessible BC Act 2021 | In force; plans and feedback mechanisms |
| Quebec | Act to ensure handicapped persons in the exercise of their rights | Long-standing; updated requirements |

---

## Practical Impact

For organizations operating in Canada:

- **Federal businesses** (banks, telecom, airlines): ACA federal requirements apply
- **Ontario operations (50+ employees):** WCAG 2.0 Level AA required
- **Best practice:** Target WCAG 2.1 Level AA — exceeds all current Canadian requirements and aligns with international standards
