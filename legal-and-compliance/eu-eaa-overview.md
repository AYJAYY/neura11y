# EU European Accessibility Act (EAA) Overview

**IMPORTANT:** Legal information changes. Verify deadlines and applicability with current official EU sources. Last reviewed: 2026-03-13.

---

## What Is the EAA?

The European Accessibility Act (EU Directive 2019/882) is a directive requiring EU member states to mandate accessibility for a wide range of products and services. It entered into force June 28, 2019.

**Key deadline:** June 28, 2025 — the date by which member states must apply the requirements to new products and services (transitional periods may apply for existing contracts).

---

## Scope: What's Covered

The EAA covers both products and services. For digital contexts:

### Products

- Computers and operating systems
- Smartphones and tablets
- TV and set-top boxes
- e-readers
- ATM machines and payment terminals
- Self-service terminals (ticketing kiosks, check-in machines)

### Services

- **E-commerce** — Online shops selling products or services
- **Banking services** — Mobile banking apps, online banking, payment services
- **Electronic communications** — VoIP, messaging, video calls
- **Media services** — On-demand video and audio platforms (streaming services)
- **Transport** — Websites, apps for air, bus, rail, waterborne transport; ticketing; real-time information
- **E-books** and e-book readers

### Not Covered by EAA (covered by Web Accessibility Directive instead)

Public sector bodies (government websites, public institutions) are covered by the **EU Web Accessibility Directive (WAD, 2016/2102)**, not the EAA. The WAD has been in force since 2018.

---

## Technical Standard: EN 301 549

The EAA's accessibility requirements are defined by reference to **EN 301 549 v3.2.1** (the European standard for ICT accessibility).

For web content, EN 301 549 Chapter 9 incorporates **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** in full.

See `standards/en-301-549/en-301-549-overview.md` for details.

---

## Who Must Comply

The EAA applies to **economic operators** — companies offering products or services in the EU market:
- Manufacturers of covered products
- Importers and distributors of covered products
- Service providers

**SME Exception:** Small and medium enterprises (fewer than 10 employees AND annual turnover/balance sheet ≤ €2M) are exempt from EAA requirements for services. For products, microenterprises (< 10 employees and ≤ €2M turnover) are exempt.

---

## The "Disproportionate Burden" Limitation

Businesses may claim an exemption where compliance would create a "disproportionate burden" or fundamentally alter the product/service. This is a case-by-case assessment, not a blanket exception.

Factors considered:
- Size of the organization
- Cost of compliance
- Estimated benefit to users with disabilities
- Frequency of use by persons with disabilities

---

## Enforcement

Each EU member state must:
- Designate market surveillance authorities
- Establish penalties for non-compliance
- Create complaint mechanisms

Penalties vary by member state but must be "effective, proportionate and dissuasive."

## Relationship to Other EU Accessibility Laws

| Law | Scope | Technical Standard |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| EAA (Directive 2019/882) | Private sector products and services | EN 301 549 |
| Web Accessibility Directive (2016/2102) | Public sector websites and apps | WCAG 2.1 AA (via EN 301 549) |
| EN 301 549 v3.2.1 | Technical standard for ICT | WCAG 2.1 AA (Chapter 9) |

---

## Practical Impact on Digital Products

For organizations offering digital products or services in the EU:

1. **E-commerce:** Websites and apps must meet WCAG 2.1 AA
2. **Mobile apps:** iOS and Android apps in covered sectors must be accessible
3. **Streaming services:** Must provide captions, audio description, accessible navigation
4. **Banking apps:** Must meet accessibility requirements for account management, payments
5. **Documentation:** Accessibility statements and conformance claims may be required

---

## Accessibility Statement Requirements

The EAA (and WAD for public bodies) requires accessibility statements including:
- A statement of compliance with the applicable standard
- Known non-compliant aspects and reasons
- Contact mechanism for reporting issues
- Link to EU enforcement body

## Country-Specific Transposition

Each EU member state implements the EAA through national law. Major implementations:

- **Germany:** Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz (BFSG) — effective June 28, 2025
- **France:** Updated Loi Handicap and accessibilité numérique requirements
- **Netherlands:** Besluit digitale toegankelijkheid overheid (primarily public sector) + EAA implementation
- **Ireland:** European Union (Accessibility Requirements for Products and Services) Regulations

Consult national law for country-specific requirements and penalties.
